# Security

Two templates and a pointer. What lives here is **doctrine**: the shape of the thinking, with no
claim about what any particular system runs.

That distinction is the whole design. A page describing your actual controls is also a page
describing your actual gaps, and the two cannot be separated by removing names. Keep the filled-in
versions in a private clone; keep the empty ones here.

- [[defense-in-depth-model]] — the eight-layer coverage checklist, plus the method for mapping
  controls to recognized frameworks without pretending to certify.
- [[incident-response-template]] — severity ladder, lifecycle, the recovery-levers principle, and
  the five scenarios worth a runbook.

The governance half, which owns how a finding is operated once discovered, lives with the other
standards: [[security-governance]].
