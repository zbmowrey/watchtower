# SEO Monitoring, Diagnostics & Update Response

Use this to operate SEO after pages are live.

## Do

- Review GSC on cadence.
- Track indexation, impressions, clicks, CTR, position, queries, and conversions.
- Segment drops before reacting.
- Use the traffic-drop decision tree.
- Log algorithm-update responses.
- Forecast clicks from ranking and SERP state.

## When

- Weekly for active SEO properties.
- Monthly for refresh and opportunity extraction.
- Immediately after traffic drops, migrations, launches, or algorithm updates.

## How

1. Confirm the drop is real.
2. Segment by page type, query group, device, country, and source.
3. Overlay releases, migrations, tracking changes, and algorithm dates.
4. Check technical/indexing issues.
5. Check SERP intent and feature changes.
6. Check content decay and competitor movement.
7. Pick the maintainer page for remediation.
8. Log action and readout date.

## Watch

- Same-weekday seasonality.
- Analytics vs GSC disagreement.
- Bot or spam distortion.
- Query intent drift.
- Indexing-state changes.
- Snippet or AI Overview changes.
- Release-correlated drops.

## Avoid

- Editing pages before segmenting the drop.
- Treating algorithm updates as a single-site verdict.
- Measuring SEO only by last-click conversions.
- Reacting to one-day changes.
