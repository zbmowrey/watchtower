# On-Page Optimization

Use this to align one URL with one intent and improve its ranking/click potential.

## Do

- Optimize title, H1, URL, headings, entities, subtopics, images, internal links, meta description, and CTA.
- Match search intent.
- Use entity coverage instead of keyword stuffing.
- Use GSC to find pages worth re-optimizing.
- Score pages with a repeatable audit.

## When

- Before publishing.
- During refresh.
- For pages with impressions and low CTR.
- For pages ranking near target range.
- After intent drift.

## How

1. Confirm target query cluster and intent.
2. Review SERP requirements.
3. Write title and H1.
4. Validate URL.
5. Add missing entities and subtopics.
6. Add internal links.
7. Optimize images.
8. Write meta description for click intent.
9. Add or update schema where relevant.
10. Measure in GSC after the window.

## Watch

- Title rewrites.
- Keyword cannibalization.
- Missing internal links.
- Overlong or unclear titles.
- Page depth mismatch with intent.
- Low CTR despite ranking.

## Avoid

- Keyword density targets.
- Changing titles too frequently.
- Optimizing pages with no impressions before higher-opportunity pages.
- Adding sections that do not serve intent.
