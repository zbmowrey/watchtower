# SEO Program Roadmap: Zero to Traction on a New Domain

Use this to decide whether SEO is the current acquisition lane and how to sequence it.

## Do

- Run the channel-fit gate.
- Start with BOFU and high-business-value pages.
- Publish in phases.
- Track leading indicators by month.
- Apply day-90, day-180, and day-365 decisions.
- Keep early SEO narrow.

## When

- Before choosing SEO as the primary lane.
- Before hiring/writing a large content batch.
- Before link outreach or programmatic SEO.
- During quarterly growth planning.

## How

1. Check demand, business value, ranking feasibility, content capacity, and time horizon.
2. Pick the first cluster.
3. Build keyword and intent map.
4. Publish BOFU assets.
5. Add supporting cluster pages.
6. Monitor indexation, impressions, query spread, rankings, clicks, and assisted conversions.
7. Decide at gates:
   - Continue.
   - Narrow.
   - Pivot.
   - Pause.

## Watch

- Pages not indexed.
- Impressions not appearing.
- Queries irrelevant to ICP.
- Content published outside cluster.
- Time spent on technical polishing before traffic exists.
- Link outreach before link-worthy assets exist.

## Avoid

- Broad TOFU content first.
- Programmatic SEO before quality gates.
- Link building before assets.
- CWV work before field data or conversion risk.
- Judging SEO from last-click only.
