# Link Acquisition & Digital PR

Use this to earn authority after there are assets worth citing.

## Do

- Build link-worthy assets first.
- Score prospects by relevance, authority, traffic, editorial standards, and relationship.
- Prioritize digital PR, data assets, reclamation, partner links, directories, and relevant mentions.
- Run personalized outreach.
- Track anchors, targets, status, and outcomes.
- Monitor link quality and velocity.

## When

- After core pages and proof assets exist.
- When ranking gap is authority, not intent/content/technical.
- During launch/directories and partnership work.
- For data, tools, research, or local/community assets.

## How

1. Select target page or asset.
2. Build prospect list.
3. Score and filter prospects.
4. Write angle and outreach.
5. Send and follow up.
6. Record link, anchor, URL, type, and relationship.
7. Measure ranking, referral, and assisted conversion.

## Watch

- Irrelevant domains.
- Paid link schemes.
- Exact-match anchor concentration.
- Sudden unnatural velocity.
- Scraper/spam links.
- Links to weak pages.

## Avoid

- Buying generic guest posts.
- Starting link building before content quality.
- Disavowing normal spam without manual-action risk.
- Measuring links by count alone.
