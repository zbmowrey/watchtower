# Keyword Research & Prioritization

Use this to choose what search demand is worth building for.

## Do

- Generate seeds from product, customers, alternatives, competitors, integrations, jobs, and sales language.
- Expand keywords with tools and SERPs.
- Cluster by SERP overlap.
- Score business value.
- Estimate traffic potential.
- Verify ranking feasibility manually.
- Prioritize by value, feasibility, and effort.

## When

- Before content production.
- Before SEO roadmap planning.
- Before pSEO.
- Quarterly for active SEO lanes.
- After positioning changes.

## How

1. List seed terms.
2. Expand variants and modifiers.
3. Pull SERPs for candidates.
4. Cluster terms that share ranking pages.
5. Assign intent from [[search-intent]].
6. Score business value 0-3.
7. Estimate traffic potential from parent topic and SERP features.
8. Check difficulty against actual competitors.
9. Build the prioritized inventory.

## Watch

- Tool volume for terms with poor intent.
- High-volume keywords with no business value.
- Keyword difficulty that ignores SERP composition.
- AI Overview or rich-result click suppression.
- Cannibalization inside clusters.

## Avoid

- Choosing keywords by volume alone.
- Treating keyword text as intent.
- Creating separate pages for terms with the same SERP.
- Targeting broad informational terms before BOFU coverage.
