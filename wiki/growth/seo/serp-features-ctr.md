# SERP Features & Organic CTR Engineering

Use this to increase clicks from existing rankings and account for SERP layout.

## Do

- Review SERP features for target queries.
- Estimate click opportunity after features.
- Write titles for relevance and click intent.
- Write meta descriptions as ad copy.
- Optimize snippets, dates, breadcrumbs, favicons, and sitelinks where possible.
- Use GSC query/page pairs for CTR tests.

## When

- When impressions exist but CTR is low.
- Before prioritizing SEO refreshes.
- After AI Overview, snippet, map, video, or shopping features appear.
- During monthly GSC review.

## How

1. Pull GSC pages with impressions.
2. Filter query/page pairs with enough data.
3. Inspect live SERP.
4. Identify feature impact.
5. Rewrite title/meta or snippet structure.
6. Wait for recrawl and data window.
7. Compare CTR, clicks, and average position.

## Watch

- CTR changes caused by rank movement.
- Google title rewrites.
- AI Overviews suppressing clicks.
- Snippet capture that lowers page clicks.
- Date displays making content look stale.

## Avoid

- Rewriting titles without enough impressions.
- Clickbait that breaks intent match.
- Measuring CTR tests over too short a window.
- Ignoring SERP features when forecasting clicks.
