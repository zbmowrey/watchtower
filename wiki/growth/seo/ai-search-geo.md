# AI Search Visibility (GEO/AEO)

Use this to make pages easier for AI answer engines to retrieve, quote, and cite.

## Do

- Keep entities clear.
- Answer concrete questions directly.
- Add citation-worthy facts, tables, comparisons, pricing, and definitions.
- Make pages crawlable to desired bots.
- Use structured data where it matches visible content.
- Track AI referrals and citation presence separately.

## When

- After core SEO basics are in place.
- For pricing, comparison, use-case, alternative, FAQ, and category pages.
- When answer engines cite competitors.
- During quarterly visibility review.

## How

1. List high-value prompts/questions.
2. Map each to an existing or needed page.
3. Add concise answer blocks.
4. Add supporting detail and proof.
5. Add source/citation links where appropriate.
6. Confirm crawl access policy.
7. Test prompts in target answer engines.
8. Track citations, referrals, and conversions.

## Watch

- Bot access blocked unintentionally.
- Thin answer blocks with no proof.
- Brand/entity ambiguity.
- AI referrals hidden under referrer noise.
- Pages cited but not clicked.

## Avoid

- Creating separate AI-only pages.
- Adding unsupported claims for answer extraction.
- Blocking all bots without a policy decision.
- Treating llms.txt as a ranking lever.
