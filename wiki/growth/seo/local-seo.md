# Local SEO & Google Business Profile

Use this for physical-location visibility and local-pack conversion.

## Do

- Complete Google Business Profile.
- Keep NAP consistent.
- Add real photos, services, categories, hours, attributes, and booking/call links.
- Build local landing pages.
- Use LocalBusiness schema.
- Run a review engine.
- Track geo-grid rankings, calls, forms, bookings, and visits.

## When

- For gyms and physical-service businesses.
- Before local paid campaigns.
- After location, hours, service, or phone changes.
- When local-pack visibility or calls drop.

## How

1. Audit GBP fields.
2. Fix categories, services, hours, NAP, photos, and links.
3. Add UTM-tagged profile links using [[analytics-stack]] vocabulary.
4. Build/refresh local landing page.
5. Add visible NAP and LocalBusiness schema.
6. Create review ask trigger and staff script.
7. Audit citations.
8. Monitor local rankings and conversions.

## Watch

- NAP drift.
- Duplicate listings.
- Suspended or unverified GBP fields.
- Review velocity spikes.
- Competitor spam.
- Stock photos.
- Local pages without real local content.

## Avoid

- Keyword stuffing business names.
- Incentivized reviews without compliance.
- City pages with no real service relevance.
- Tracking local rank from one point only.
