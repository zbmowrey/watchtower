# How Search Works & Ranking Systems

Use this to diagnose whether a search problem is crawl, render, index, rank, or click.

## Do

- Classify every SEO issue by pipeline stage.
- Check crawlability.
- Check renderability.
- Check index eligibility.
- Check canonical and duplicate signals.
- Check query intent and serving/ranking.
- Use [[seo-monitoring]] for operating response.

## When

- Before changing content after a traffic drop.
- Before technical SEO fixes.
- Before migration forensics.
- When pages fail to rank or appear in GSC.

## How

1. Confirm URL returns the correct status.
2. Confirm robots, noindex, canonical, and redirects.
3. Confirm rendered HTML includes primary content and links.
4. Confirm sitemap and internal links expose the URL.
5. Inspect GSC indexing state.
6. Compare SERP intent and competing pages.
7. Assign next owner: [[technical-seo]], [[site-architecture]], [[content-production]], [[on-page-seo]], or [[seo-monitoring]].

## Watch

- JavaScript content missing in rendered HTML.
- Canonical pointing away from target URL.
- Crawled but not indexed.
- Duplicate pages competing.
- SERP intent changing.
- Algorithm-update timing.

## Avoid

- Treating all ranking loss as content quality.
- Fixing titles before index eligibility.
- Blocking URLs in robots.txt to remove them from the index.
- Diagnosing algorithm updates without segmenting pages and queries.
