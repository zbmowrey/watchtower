# Search Intent Classification & Mapping

Use this to decide what asset can rank for a query.

## Do

- Classify intent from the SERP.
- Map each intent to one asset type.
- Detect mixed intent.
- Track intent drift.
- Update pages when Google changes the SERP shape.

## When

- During keyword research.
- Before writing briefs.
- Before refreshing or merging content.
- When rankings drop without technical issues.

## How

1. Search the query.
2. Record top-ranking page types.
3. Record SERP features.
4. Label intent:
   - Informational.
   - Commercial investigation.
   - Transactional.
   - Navigational.
   - Local.
   - Mixed.
5. Choose asset type.
6. Mark required sections and proof.
7. Recheck priority queries on cadence.

## Watch

- SERPs split between listicles, product pages, docs, and local results.
- Google replacing organic results with AI, snippets, maps, or videos.
- Ranking pages from a different business model.
- Query modifiers that change intent.

## Avoid

- Building a blog post for a product-page SERP.
- Building a sales page for an informational SERP.
- Combining conflicting intents on one URL.
- Keeping a page unchanged after intent drift.
