# Topical Authority & Content Clusters

Use this to build coherent coverage around a core entity or job.

## Do

- Define the core entity.
- Map subtopics, queries, and assets.
- Pick pillar and cluster pages.
- Link clusters deliberately.
- Measure coverage, indexation, rankings, and query spread.
- Expand only after core coverage is strong.

## When

- After keyword research.
- Before sustained content production.
- Before expanding into adjacent topics.
- During quarterly SEO planning.

## How

1. Choose the core topic.
2. List subtopics and jobs.
3. Map queries to subtopics.
4. Cluster by SERP overlap.
5. Assign pillar, cluster, comparison, integration, glossary, or support asset.
6. Publish in dependency order.
7. Link cluster pages to pillar and siblings.
8. Audit competitor coverage.
9. Fill gaps or prune weak overlap.

## Watch

- Thin clusters with no money-page path.
- Pages covering adjacent topics before the core topic is complete.
- Internal links that do not reinforce the target page.
- Coverage without clicks or business value.
- Multiple pages targeting one intent.

## Avoid

- Publishing isolated posts.
- Measuring authority by page count.
- Expanding sideways before coverage reaches the planned threshold.
- Treating topical maps as permanent.
