# Content Decay, Refresh & Pruning

Use this to recover or remove declining content.

## Do

- Detect decay with rolling click, impression, and position trends.
- Score refresh priority by traffic at stake, recovery likelihood, business value, and effort.
- Choose update, merge, leave, redirect, noindex, or delete.
- Preserve winning URLs where possible.
- Measure recovery after refresh.

## When

- Monthly for active SEO properties.
- After algorithm updates.
- When traffic drops by page group.
- Before publishing more content in an overlapping cluster.

## How

1. Pull last 90 days and previous comparable period.
2. Flag declining pages.
3. Segment by intent, cluster, URL type, and business value.
4. Inspect SERP and page quality.
5. Pick action:
   - Update.
   - Merge.
   - Leave.
   - Prune.
6. Implement redirects/canonicals/internal links.
7. Re-submit and monitor.

## Watch

- Seasonality.
- SERP intent drift.
- Cannibalization.
- Lost internal links.
- Outdated proof, screenshots, pricing, or policy.
- Pages with clicks but no business value.

## Avoid

- Refreshing every old page.
- Deleting URLs with backlinks or conversions without a redirect plan.
- Merging pages with different intent.
- Calling decay from too short a window.
