# Content Distribution & Repurposing

Use this to make every asset reach its intended audience after publication.

## Do

- Plan distribution before publishing.
- Create owned, borrowed, and earned distribution actions.
- Repurpose each pillar asset into channel-native formats.
- Seed communities with context.
- Schedule reshares.
- Track UTMs, referrals, assisted conversions, and qualitative replies.

## When

- For every major content asset.
- Before launch campaigns.
- Before social/email/community pushes.
- When content gets impressions but little downstream action.

## How

1. Define audience and primary CTA.
2. Choose channels.
3. Create snippets and native versions.
4. Add UTM links.
5. Publish owned channel.
6. Send email where relevant.
7. Post native social versions.
8. Seed communities if allowed.
9. Reshare on cadence.
10. Measure traffic, saves, replies, links, leads, and conversions.

## Watch

- Community self-promo rules.
- Link penalties or suppression.
- Dark social that will not show as last click.
- Asset decay after first share.
- Reused hooks in owned-asset reshares.

## Avoid

- Publishing without distribution tasks.
- Dumping links into communities.
- Reusing identical copy across platforms.
- Measuring only immediate clicks.
- Treating distribution as optional.
