# Organic Social & Founder Brand

Use this for founder-led attention, trust, and DM pipeline.

## Do

- Use founder accounts for early-stage distribution.
- Pick 2-3 content pillars.
- Post in platform-native formats.
- Engage before and after posting.
- Move qualified conversations to DM.
- Track dark-social signals.

## When

- When founder/operator can post consistently.
- Before brand account investment.
- Around launches, content distribution, proof collection, and market learning.
- When audience trust matters before purchase.

## How

1. Choose platforms by ICP presence and operator capacity.
2. Define pillars:
   - Problem insight.
   - Build/proof.
   - Customer/use-case.
3. Convert long-form assets into native posts.
4. Post without forcing external links where the platform suppresses them.
5. Reply and DM relevant commenters.
6. Log mentions, DMs, self-reported attribution, branded/direct lift, and leads.

## Watch

- High engagement from non-buyers.
- Link suppression.
- Founder capacity.
- Format drift by platform.
- DMs that never enter CRM or lead log.

## Avoid

- Brand-only posting at zero audience.
- Posting links without native value.
- Measuring only likes.
- Treating viral reach as pipeline.
- Opening too many platforms at once.
