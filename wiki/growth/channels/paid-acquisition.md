# Paid Acquisition for Small SaaS

Use this only after unit economics and the landing path can support spend.

## Do

- Define target CAC and payback.
- Calculate minimum viable test budget.
- Start with high-intent search where possible.
- Keep campaign structure narrow.
- Track lead quality, activation, paid conversion, and CAC.
- Use holdouts or incrementality checks when spend matters.

## When

- LTV/payback is known or bounded.
- Landing page and conversion tracking are verified.
- Organic/owned channels cannot answer demand fast enough.
- Local business has a trackable offer and conversion path.

## How

1. Set target CAC.
2. Estimate CPC, CVR, and test budget.
3. Choose channel:
   - Google Search for captured intent.
   - Meta for local/offers/retargeting.
   - LinkedIn only for high-ACV narrow B2B.
4. Build one landing page per intent.
5. Launch small test.
6. Review search terms, lead quality, activation, and paid conversion.
7. Kill, narrow, or ramp.

## Watch

- Platforms optimizing to low-quality leads.
- PMax lead-gen traps.
- Consent-mode data loss.
- Retargeting pool size.
- CPA below target but CAC above target after quality.
- Budget ramp changing traffic quality.

## Avoid

- Paid before analytics and revenue truth.
- Broad match without controls.
- Scaling from form-fill CPA alone.
- Mixing prospecting and retargeting readouts.
- Spending past the predeclared test limit.
