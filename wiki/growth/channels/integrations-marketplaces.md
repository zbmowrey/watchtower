# Integration Marketplaces & Partner Distribution

Use this to borrow distribution from platforms your ICP already uses.

## Do

- Select marketplaces by audience, intent, build cost, ranking path, and retention loop.
- Build only integrations that create real product value.
- Optimize listing title, category, screenshots, proof, docs, and support path.
- Track installs, activation, retention, and referred revenue.
- Pursue co-marketing where value exists.

## When

- Target users already search inside a platform marketplace.
- Integration improves activation or retention.
- Build/support cost is lower than expected acquisition value.
- Product has enough reliability for partner exposure.

## How

1. List candidate platforms.
2. Score audience fit, search demand, competition, build effort, support load, and revenue potential.
3. Build minimal useful integration.
4. Prepare listing and docs.
5. Add install and activation tracking.
6. Launch listing.
7. Request co-marketing or partner placement.
8. Review keep/kill by installs, activation, retention, and support cost.

## Watch

- Marketplace ranking factors.
- App review delays.
- Support burden.
- API changes.
- Low install-to-activation.
- Powered-by loops that create low-quality leads.

## Avoid

- Building integrations for logos only.
- Listing without onboarding.
- Depending on a platform without monitoring policy/API changes.
- Counting installs as customers.
