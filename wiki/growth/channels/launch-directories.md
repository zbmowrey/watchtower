# Launches, Directories & Review Platforms

Use this to get initial visibility, links, users, reviews, and market feedback.

## Do

- Prepare launch assets.
- Pick launch platforms and directories by product fit.
- Submit long-tail profiles.
- Ask customers for compliant reviews.
- Track referral traffic, links, signups, reviews, and assisted conversions.
- Maintain profile accuracy.

## When

- When product is listable and the landing page converts.
- During launch month.
- After meaningful product positioning changes.
- When review proof is the constraint.

## How

1. Build launch kit: name, tagline, description, screenshots, founder note, offer, URL, UTMs.
2. Pick primary launch day.
3. Submit directories in batches.
4. Claim review profiles.
5. Ask eligible customers for reviews.
6. Record published URLs, DR/link type, referral traffic, and conversions.
7. Refresh profiles quarterly.

## Watch

- Nofollow links.
- Low-quality directories.
- Review-platform rule changes.
- Incentive disclosure.
- Launch traffic that does not activate.

## Avoid

- Launching before page and analytics work.
- Expecting Product Hunt alone to create durable growth.
- Paying for directory listings without tracking.
- Copying inconsistent descriptions across profiles.
