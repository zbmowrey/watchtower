# Niche Sponsorships: Newsletters, Podcasts & Communities

Use this for flat-rate placements into a concentrated audience.

## Do

- Calculate max affordable placement price.
- Verify audience fit.
- Check real engagement, not media-kit claims.
- Negotiate placement, makegoods, exclusivity, and reporting.
- Use UTM and promo codes.
- Predefine kill/scale criteria.

## When

- Audience is concentrated in a niche publication/community/show.
- Paid auction CPCs do not clear CAC math.
- Offer and landing page are proven enough for a placement test.

## How

1. Define target CAC and expected conversion.
2. Compute max price per placement.
3. Build inventory list.
4. Verify traffic, list quality, comments, sponsor history, and audience overlap.
5. Negotiate terms.
6. Provide creative matched to audience.
7. Track clicks, signups/leads, paid conversion, and self-reported attribution.
8. Decide repeat, negotiate, or stop.

## Watch

- Apple MPP distorting email metrics.
- Inflated subscriber counts.
- Audience mismatch.
- No landing-page message match.
- Results credited only by last click.

## Avoid

- Buying from rate card without affordability math.
- Running one-off creative with no tracking.
- Sponsoring broad audiences.
- Repeating placements without cohort quality.
