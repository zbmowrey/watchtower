# Cold Outbound for Micro-SaaS

Use this for narrow B2B acquisition where ACV supports manual prospecting.

## Do

- Pass the ACV and funnel-math gate.
- Use dedicated sending infrastructure.
- Build verified prospect lists.
- Personalize by trigger or fit.
- Use 3-4 touch sequences.
- Check compliance by jurisdiction.
- Stop or repair when reply/positive/meeting rates miss the gate.

## When

- ICP is narrow and identifiable.
- Contract value supports human effort.
- Positioning and offer are clear.
- Warm channels are not enough to reach target accounts.

## How

1. Define ICP and trigger.
2. Build 50-prospect test list.
3. Verify emails and company fit.
4. Write sequence:
   - Problem/trigger.
   - Relevant proof.
   - Specific ask.
   - Breakup or alternate value.
5. Configure sending domain and warmup.
6. Send at controlled volume.
7. Track delivered, reply, positive reply, meeting, close, and spam.
8. Hand off warm replies quickly.

## Watch

- Spam complaints.
- Burned domains.
- Bad list quality.
- Generic personalization.
- Low positive replies despite opens.
- Compliance rules by geography.

## Avoid

- Scaling before the 50-prospect test works.
- Sending from primary domain.
- Buying broad lists.
- Automating follow-up after a human reply.
- Treating cold outbound as a low-ACV channel.
