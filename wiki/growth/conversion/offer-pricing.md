# Offer Design & Pricing Pages

Use this before pricing pages, checkout, trials, guarantees, discounts, or paid acquisition.

## Do

- Define the offer before designing the page.
- State the buyer, outcome, mechanism, risk reversal, and next step.
- Choose pricing model: flat, tiered, seat, usage, hybrid, service, or custom.
- Create tiers by value and cost driver.
- Anchor the recommended plan.
- Choose trial, freemium, demo, or direct checkout deliberately.
- Define discount rules and approval limits.
- Monitor conversion, ARPA, churn, and payback after changes.

## When

- Before publishing pricing.
- Before paid acquisition.
- Before changing trial/freemium/demo.
- When conversion quality or payback is weak.
- When support or sales sees price confusion.

## How

1. Define the value equation.
2. Pick the pricing axis.
3. Create 2-4 plan options.
4. Assign features by buyer maturity and cost.
5. Add proof and guarantee.
6. Add FAQ for price objections.
7. Connect checkout/demo to the correct next step.
8. Instrument plan views, toggle use, checkout starts, checkout completion, downgrades, and churn.

## Watch

- Annual discount hurting payback less than expected or more than expected.
- Users choosing the wrong plan.
- High trial starts with low activation.
- Discounts creating low-retention cohorts.
- Usage costs exceeding flat-plan margins.

## Avoid

- Pricing by internal effort only.
- Feature tables that hide the recommended action.
- Per-seat pricing when the value metric is not seats.
- Guarantees that support cannot honor.
- Changing price and positioning in one unmeasured move.
