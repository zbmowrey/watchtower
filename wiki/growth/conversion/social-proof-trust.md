# Social Proof & Trust Engineering

Use this to make claims credible.

## Do

- Collect proof immediately after value delivery.
- Prefer quantified customer outcomes.
- Use named testimonials where permission exists.
- Build case studies from situation, trigger, barrier, action, result.
- Display reviews and ratings with current, compliant language.
- Add trust signals for unknown brands.
- Follow disclosure and incentive rules.

## When

- Before cold traffic.
- Before pricing pages.
- Before launch/directories/review-platform work.
- When users believe the promise but hesitate.
- After customer milestones.

## How

1. Identify proof gaps by page and objection.
2. Trigger testimonial/review ask after success moments.
3. Ask for before state, chosen alternative, reason for switch, result, and permission.
4. Store proof with source, date, permission, and approved display.
5. Place proof near the claim it supports.
6. Refresh stale or unrepresentative proof.

## Watch

- Anonymous quotes on high-trust pages.
- Logo walls without context.
- Incentivized reviews without disclosure.
- Outlier outcomes presented as typical.
- Review counts or ratings that drift.
- Proof that does not match the target ICP.

## Avoid

- Fabricating or editing customer meaning.
- Hiding material connections.
- Using generic "loved by teams" claims.
- Putting proof only at the bottom of the page.
- Asking for referrals/reviews before value delivery.
