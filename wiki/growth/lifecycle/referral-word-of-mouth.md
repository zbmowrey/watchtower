# Referral & Word-of-Mouth Programs

Use this after customers reliably get value and stay.

## Do

- Confirm retention readiness.
- Define referral event and reward event.
- Choose incentive type.
- Ask after value moments.
- Track referred users separately.
- Add fraud checks.
- Monitor K-factor, cycle time, CAC equivalent, and retention.

## When

- After retention curve flattens.
- After satisfaction signal is positive.
- After activation and payment are working.
- When a natural sharing moment exists.

## How

1. Pick eligible referrers.
2. Pick eligible referred customers.
3. Define reward trigger.
4. Create referral links or codes.
5. Add credit ledger.
6. Add in-product ask at success moments.
7. Add email/share copy.
8. Review fraud and cohort quality.

## Watch

- Rewards paid before real customer quality.
- Self-referrals.
- Coupon abuse.
- Referred users with lower retention.
- Incentives that attract the wrong segment.
- Affiliate disclosure requirements.

## Avoid

- Launching referral to fix weak retention.
- Asking immediately after signup.
- Overpaying before LTV is known.
- Rewarding activity instead of qualified conversion.
- Hiding material incentives.
