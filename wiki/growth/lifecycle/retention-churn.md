# Retention, Churn & Expansion

Use this to keep customers and grow account value.

## Do

- Read retention curves by cohort.
- Track logo churn, revenue churn, GRR, NRR, contraction, expansion, and reactivation.
- Segment churn by ICP, plan, channel, activation path, tenure, and reason.
- Build cancellation flows from stated reasons.
- Recover failed payments.
- Trigger expansion from usage, team, feature, or value signals.

## When

- Before referral programs.
- Before scaling paid acquisition.
- When churn, contraction, or payback is the constraint.
- After pricing, onboarding, or product changes.

## How

1. Build cohort retention tables.
2. Identify where the curve drops and whether it flattens.
3. Pull cancellation reasons.
4. Compare churned vs retained accounts.
5. Choose one churn segment.
6. Ship a save, fix, dunning, win-back, or expansion action.
7. Measure retention and revenue movement after the window.

## Watch

- Early churn tied to failed activation.
- Churn concentrated in one segment or plan.
- Involuntary churn counted as product churn.
- Expansion masking core retention failure.
- Discounts creating weak cohorts.
- Cancellation reasons that do not match behavior.

## Avoid

- Referral before retention readiness.
- Generic save offers.
- Making cancellation hostile.
- Averaging churn across incompatible segments.
- Calling NRR healthy while GRR is weak.
