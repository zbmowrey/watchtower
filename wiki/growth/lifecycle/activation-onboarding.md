# Activation & Onboarding

Use this to get new users to first value quickly.

## Do

- Define activation as a value event.
- Split onboarding into setup, aha, and habit.
- Remove steps before first value.
- Instrument time-to-value.
- Coordinate in-app and email guidance.
- Review activation weekly while it is the constraint.

## When

- Before scaling acquisition.
- When signups do not become active users.
- When trial conversion is weak.
- After major product, signup, or onboarding changes.

## How

1. List candidate activation events.
2. Compare activated and non-activated cohorts.
3. Pick the event that predicts retention or payment.
4. Map the shortest path from signup to that event.
5. Remove, defer, automate, or template every nonessential step.
6. Add prompts for the next action only.
7. Trigger onboarding emails from behavior, not fixed hope.
8. Track setup completion, aha completion, habit repeat, and TTV.

## Watch

- Account creation counted as activation.
- Empty dashboard after signup.
- Required integrations before value.
- Tooltips replacing product clarity.
- Email nudges that point to completed or irrelevant steps.
- Activation rate varying sharply by channel.

## Avoid

- Teaching the whole product in onboarding.
- Asking for configuration before showing value.
- Treating completion rate as success without retention/payment linkage.
- Adding more guidance instead of removing steps.
