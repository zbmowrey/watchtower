# Email Capture & Nurture

Use this to own follow-up with visitors who are not ready to buy.

## Do

- Offer one relevant lead magnet.
- Place capture where intent supports it.
- Set opt-in mode deliberately.
- Send a welcome sequence.
- Segment nurture by behavior.
- Maintain deliverability requirements.
- Track capture, confirmation, opens/clicks where reliable, and conversion.

## When

- When acquisition brings non-buying visitors.
- Before content distribution.
- Before launch campaigns.
- When buying cycle is longer than one visit.
- For local offers, events, class interest, or waitlists.

## How

1. Match lead magnet to page intent.
2. Define the next conversion after capture.
3. Add capture form with minimal fields.
4. Configure consent and deliverability.
5. Build sequence:
   - Deliver asset.
   - Name problem.
   - Show proof.
   - Present offer.
   - Ask for action.
6. Add behavior triggers.
7. Review list quality and conversion monthly.

## Watch

- Low-quality incentives.
- Capture modals blocking primary conversion.
- MPP-distorted open rates.
- Spam complaints.
- Deliverability/authentication failures.
- Nurture that ignores product behavior.

## Avoid

- Capturing email with no follow-up.
- Asking for too much information.
- Treating open rate as the main metric.
- Sending every subscriber the same sequence forever.
- Buying lists.
