# Watchtower Wiki

On-disk, permanent memory for engineering a set of apps to one standard. This is a
[[wiki-conventions|Karpathy-style wiki]] — **search it first** with the [[wiki-tool]]
(`bin/wiki search <kw>` → `bin/wiki inject --page <slug> --depth 1`), not the open
internet. One concept per page; directory == domain; each domain hub below lists its
own pages (generated by `bin/wiki index`). The how/why of maintaining it is the
[[tending-the-garden]] charter.

This is the **public scaffold** cut of a larger private knowledge base: it ships the
reusable, brand-neutral domains (the engineering standard, the stack manual, the wiki
system itself). The private-by-nature domains — your projects, roadmaps, infra
topology, security posture, growth, and dated logs — exist here only as **empty
structural stubs** (a `.gitkeep` and a `.gitignore` rule). The skeleton shows you where
your own content goes; you fill those folders in your own private clone. See the
[README](../README.md) for what is and isn't included.
